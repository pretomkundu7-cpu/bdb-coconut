BDB COCONUT website
====================
A responsive motion-style business website starter for BDB COCONUT.

Included:
- Home, About, Products, Quality & Production, Gallery, Contact
- Founder: Uttam Kundu
- Location: Bagerhat District, Bangladesh
- Dark green + lime premium design
- Scroll reveal animations, hover effects, responsive mobile menu

To run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser.

Before publishing, replace the placeholder email (hello@bdbcoconut.com), add real phone/WhatsApp details, and replace emoji product visuals with your real product/farm photos.
